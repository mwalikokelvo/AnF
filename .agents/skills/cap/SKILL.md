# Cap — Open Source Screen Recording & Video Sharing

Cap is an open-source, privacy-first screen recording and video messaging tool designed as a transparent alternative to Loom.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Screen & Audio Capture**
   - Record entire screen, application window, or designated display area.
   - Dual audio stream capture: system audio (stereo mix) and microphone commentary.
   - Hardware-accelerated H.264/WebM encoding with custom bitrate and resolution scaling (up to 4K 60fps).

2. **Walkthroughs & Product Demos**
   - Instant capture and export for release reviews, bug reproduction recordings, and feature demos.
   - Built-in trimming, timeline slicing, and audio normalization.
   - Zero-telemetry local recording with direct MP4/GIF/WebM export.

3. **Workflow Integration**
   - Integrate with documentation and bug tracking (GitHub Issues, PR descriptions).
   - Timestamped markers for step-by-step reproduction flows.
