# Supoclip — AI Short-Form Video & Highlights Generation

Supoclip is an open-source tool for turning long-form podcasts, webinars, and audio/video files into viral short-form clips with animated subtitles and face tracking.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Intelligent Highlight Detection**
   - Audio transcription analysis to detect key insights, punchlines, and narrative hooks.
   - Virality and engagement scoring based on speech cadence, emotion, and sentiment peaks.
   - Silence trimming, filler word deletion, and pacing optimization.

2. **Aspect Ratio Conversion & Auto-Framing**
   - Automatic 16:9 widescreen to 9:16 vertical crop with active speaker face tracking.
   - Split-screen framing (reaction + speaker) for interview podcasts.
   - Dynamic canvas padding, background blur, and branding overlays.

3. **Animated Dynamic Subtitles**
   - Word-level Whisper timestamping for karaoke-style animated captions.
   - Customizable styling (font, color, highlight, stroke, emojis, bounce effects).
   - Direct export to MP4 ready for social media publishing.
