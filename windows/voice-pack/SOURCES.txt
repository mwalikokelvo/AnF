Kevo voice pack
===============

The 13 recordings here are built into the app: Vite serves them at /voice-pack/*.mp3 in dev and
copies them into dist/voice-pack/ on build, so every installer and portable folder carries them.
File names must match windows/src/workspace/jarvis/voice/jarvis-voice-pack.ts.

The owner made or designed these voices and chose to publish all 13 with the app (14 Nov 2026).
"Kevo - gentle bass.mp3" is the owner's own voice.

What they are used for: the "Hear this voice" button plays a recording in full, and the first second
is the chime Kevo plays when it hears its name. Kevo's replies are spoken by a Windows voice matched
to the chosen profile's accent and gender; the recordings are not a speech engine.
