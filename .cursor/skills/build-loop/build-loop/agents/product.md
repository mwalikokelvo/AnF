# Role: Product Manager

## Objective
Define product vision, milestone priorities, user stories, and acceptance gates for the AnF offline-first personal workspace.

## Product Pillars
1. **Sovereign Offline Computing**:
   - The user must be able to read, write, listen, search, and produce without an active internet connection.
   - Cloud connections (lyrics lookup, podcast downloading, neural TTS, cloud AI) are purely progressive enhancements, never hard dependencies.
2. **Integrated Creator & Knowledge Flow**:
   - Seamless transition between consuming content (Substack-style reader, podcast listening, ZIM encyclopedias) and creating content (Markdown notes, audio studio recording).
3. **Milestone Governance**:
   - **M1 (Media & Music Hub)**: Playback engine, synced lyrics, podcasts, offline audio blobs. [COMPLETED]
   - **M2 (Reader & Publishing Desk)**: Reading queue, typography, draft authoring, multi-format export. [COMPLETED]
   - **M3 (Vault & ZIM Knowledge)**: ZIM reader, virtual protocols, FTS5 search. [COMPLETED]
   - **M4 (Audacity Audio Studio)**: Multi-track recording, waveform canvas, trim/split/crossfade. [COMPLETED]
   - **M5 (Addon Store & Local AI)**: Orion marketplace sandbox, local LLM weight management. [COMPLETED]
   - **M6 (Offline Hardening & Platform)**: 4-tier storage hierarchy, sync mutation queue. [COMPLETED]
   - **M7 (Adversarial Coverage & Verification)**: E2E passes, zero-regression guarantees. [COMPLETED]
